# Code of Conduct / 行為準則

本專案採用 [Contributor Covenant 貢獻者公約 2.1 版](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
作為我們的行為準則，期望所有參與者共同營造開放、友善、多元且互相尊重的社群環境。

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its Code of Conduct. We expect everyone who participates to help maintain an open, welcoming,
diverse, and respectful community.

## 摘要 / In short

- 以善意與尊重對待彼此，歡迎不同背景與觀點的參與者。
- Treat others with good faith and respect; participants of all backgrounds and viewpoints are welcome.
- 對人對事皆採取建設性的溝通方式，並接受社群的建設性回饋。
- Communicate constructively and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- 不容許騷擾、人身攻擊或任何使他人感到不受歡迎的行為。
- Harassment, personal attacks, or any conduct that makes others feel unwelcome are not tolerated.

完整條款請見上方連結的官方公約全文（亦有[繁體中文翻譯](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-tw/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)）。

For the complete terms, please read the official text linked above
(a [Traditional Chinese translation](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/zh-tw/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
is also available).

## 通報 / Reporting

如遇到違反行為準則的情況，請來信 [volunteer@ocf.tw](mailto:volunteer@ocf.tw) 通報。
所有通報都會被審慎、保密地處理。

To report unacceptable behavior, please email [volunteer@ocf.tw](mailto:volunteer@ocf.tw).
All reports will be reviewed and handled discreetly and confidentially.
